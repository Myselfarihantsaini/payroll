India’s four labour codes.

A starting point for reviewing workforce records, payroll processes and compliance responsibilities.

IN THIS GUIDEThe four codesOperational reviewOfficial sourceMore regulatory sources

Reviewed: 10 September 2026 · India · General information

What changed?

The Government of India announced that the four labour codes took effect on 21 November 2025, consolidating 29 central labour laws. The announcement also describes transitional arrangements. Check the latest rules and notifications for your specific establishment.

Code on Wages, 2019

Wages, payment practices and related payroll records.

Industrial Relations Code, 2020

Employment relations and related workplace procedures.

Code on Social Security, 2020

Social security arrangements and associated workforce records.

Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020

Safety, working conditions and establishment responsibilities.

A practical review for HR and Finance

Use these questions to organise an assessment, rather than as a complete legal compliance checklist.

  1. Map the workforce. Identify employing entities, establishments, states, employee categories and contractor arrangements.
  2. Review payroll inputs. Bring salary components, attendance, overtime and settlement processes into the review.
  3. Check supporting records. Locate appointment documents, registers, contribution records and contractor evidence.
  4. Assign decisions and actions. Record which questions need legal interpretation, who owns each action and what evidence will close it.
  5. Communicate agreed changes. Align HR, Finance, site teams and contractors before implementing revised processes.

Rates, eligibility, thresholds and deadlines depend on applicable provisions and notifications. Obtain advice for your circumstances before changing payroll or employment policies.

Ministry of Labour & Employment announcement, published by PIB on 25 November 2025
